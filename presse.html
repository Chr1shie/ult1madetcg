Was Ult1made TCG besonders macht

  • 15 Spiele, eine App. Statt pro Spiel eine eigene App – alles in einer Sammlung, mit einheitlicher Bedienung.
  • Kamera-Erkennung an echten Karten getestet. Jedes Spiel hat seine eigene Erkennungslogik, deutsche Drucke und Foils eingeschlossen.
  • Binder wie im Regal. Gleiche Seitengröße, gleiche Reihenfolge, Umblättern per Wisch – der echte Binder wird 1:1 digital.
  • Regelcheck beim Deckbau. Standard, Pioneer und Commander bei Magic inklusive Farbidentität, dazu Pokémon, Dragon Ball und Final Fantasy – offline.
  • Eigene Daten, eigener Server. Kein Konto, keine Anbieter-Cloud. Der Begleit-Server ist quelloffen und läuft auf Raspberry Pi, NAS oder jedem Linux-Rechner.
  • Holo-Effekte. Foil-Karten reagieren im Vollbild auf das Gyroskop – Regenbogen, Galaxie, Gold, Glitzer.

  • Fertige Magic-Decks aus dem Vault ins Deckbau-Tool übernehmen; Import aus ManaBox und Decklisten aus Arena, Moxfield oder Archidekt.
  • Deutsche Kartenbilder für Pokémon, Magic, Final Fantasy und Altered.
  • Binder neu einscannen: Karten landen in Scan-Reihenfolge, einzeln oder im Massenscan.
  • Accounts zusammenführen, Freischalt-Code per QR-Code, Foto drehen im Vault.

Short text (about 60 words)

Ult1made TCG brings 15 trading card games into one app – from Pokémon and Magic to One Piece, Lorcana and Final Fantasy. Cards are recognized by camera, binders rebuilt 1:1, decks built with a rules check, and values tracked with real Cardmarket prices. No account and no third-party cloud: data stays on the device or on your own open-source server. On the App Store since September 7, 2026.

Long text (about 150 words)

Collectors of several trading card games have needed several apps so far. Ult1made TCG unites 15 of them: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Disney Lorcana, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Digimon, Dragon Ball Fusion World, Final Fantasy, Flesh and Blood, Star Wars: Unlimited, Altered, Riftbound, Gundam, Cyberpunk and Naruto Kayou.

Cards are captured with the camera – recognition is tuned per game against real cards, including German prints and foil variants. Bulk scan takes whole stacks, a review screen shows every card before saving. Binders can be rebuilt exactly like the real album with 4 to 16 pockets per page, decks get a rules check for Pokémon, Dragon Ball, Magic (Standard, Pioneer, Commander) and Final Fantasy. Values are based on real Cardmarket prices in euro.

The core principle: no accounts, no vendor cloud. Everything lives on the device; optionally a free, open-source server on your own hardware syncs all devices and serves a web UI for the big screen. One TCG is free forever, Premium unlocks all games.

What makes Ult1made TCG different

  • 15 games, one app. Instead of one app per game – everything in one collection with one consistent UI.
  • Camera recognition tested on real cards. Every game has its own recognition logic, German prints and foils included.
  • Binders like on your shelf. Same page size, same order, flip pages with a swipe – the real binder goes digital 1:1.
  • Rules check while deck building. Standard, Pioneer and Commander for Magic including color identity, plus Pokémon, Dragon Ball and Final Fantasy – offline.
  • Your data, your server. No account, no vendor cloud. The companion server is open source and runs on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any Linux box.
  • Holo effects. Foil cards react to the gyroscope in fullscreen – Rainbow, Galaxy, Gold, Glitter.

New in version 1.1

  • Deck building with rules check for Magic (Standard, Pioneer, Commander) and Final Fantasy, decks as 3D boxes with a cover.
  • Take preconstructed Magic decks from the vault into the deck builder; import from ManaBox and deck lists from Arena, Moxfield or Archidekt.
  • German card images for Pokémon, Magic, Final Fantasy and Altered.
  • Re-scan a binder: cards land in scan order, single or bulk.
  • Merge accounts, unlock codes via QR code, rotate photos in the vault.
